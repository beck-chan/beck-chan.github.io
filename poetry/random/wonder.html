Wonder

what a Wonder it is
to Know

the sunrises You were always meant to see
the winds across that whirling, raging ocean
from which You clambered
with fang
 and claw
  and
a soft, capricious Heart

what a Wonder it is
to Know

each drop of rain by teasing touch
every incandescent star,
 fleeting dream,
  vainglorious wish
hung so desperately upon the mantle of the sky

what a Wonder it is
to Know
the silence still upon a lake,
the flicker of frost
courting the sentinel of
 Autumn’s last citadel
    then,

the sanguine taste of Spring’s
first audacious blooms

what a Wonder it is
to Know

when that sea comes once again to claim
 what has always and
  will always be Yours
   as it has been since you were You
       who asked

Oh

how?
  how is it

To be?

   A Wonder.
