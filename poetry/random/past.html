past

It seems so far away, this memory of mine:
just shy of a decade
 old
& yet—
it feels like
 a lifetime

  away.

Certainly
the you & I
from that era are
 dead

One day
we play hide-&-seek
for the last time
 before
parting ways for
good
our hopes & aspirations
 tucked away
in a corner of a secret
garden
  never

to be found.

Strange
 isn’t it

That we hold such elaborate funerals for the lives that were lived
 & then lost
but for the lives that were lost
before they were ever
 lived
we say to ourselves that there is no use in mourning
what was not meant

  to be.

Stranger still
every day we say an eerily nonchalant goodbye to
 yesterday
to the echo of our souls
now buried by the sands of time—
in tomorrow
there is no room
  for

   the past.

No graves are dug
no rites are performed
for that which will never be
 again

We simply
enclose our grief carefully
in a coffin deep within
 our hearts
in a mausoleum of memories
we visit only
on the nights
that are too quiet
 &
  too

   long.

When we look up
at the painfully fulgent moon
 round & full
in the relentless dark
in its shadows
 we see
the vestiges
  of
   days

    bygone
